# WorkHub Praxis and resource planning

WorkHub uses Overview, Projects, Praxis and TechShare. Existing dashboard preferences, project records, documents, work contributions and published knowledge remain in their original stores. Legacy performance and contributor routes remain compatible. Testing tools and validation engines are unchanged by this upgrade.

## Set up people and teams

An Admin opens Settings > Departments, roles & teams > Manage teams and user details. Create departments and professional roles, then teams. Choose existing users as members and mark the team administrators. A team administrator can assess and configure models for their managed departments; this does not grant the application's Admin sign-in role.

Team Details appears under My profile and each user in Users & Roles. Admin records the department, professional role, reporting manager, teams, weekly capacity and availability. Reporting relationships cannot form cycles. Planning notes are visible to the person and authorized managers. The default 40-hour week is an explicit planning assumption until configured.

## Plan resources

Open Projects > Task Management. Filter the period, project, department, team or availability. A person's capacity includes allocations from other projects even when a single project is selected. Native open leaf tasks supply responsibility, dates, remaining estimates and blockers. Completed parent tasks are not double-counted. Overdue open work and incomplete estimates remain unknown rather than appearing as free time. Review-stage effort is not inferred from another person's development estimate.

Select a person to see their responsibilities. Open the original task or use Assign / reassign, subject to the original native task authorization and version checks. Capacity and workload are planning figures, not performance scores.

## Configure Praxis

Admin creates company assessment periods. Admin or authorized department managers configure competencies and role models through Configure Praxis. Definitions include a category, description, criteria and review frequency. Role models set required proficiency, measurement weights and role-specific performance KPIs/KRAs. No engineering competency set or production scores are seeded.

The default levels are Awareness, Basic, Proficient, Advanced and Expert. Performance calculations can use a weighted arithmetic mean, weighted geometric mean or lowest applicable KPI. KPI criteria explain how the supported 0–100 reviewer measurement is established. The Competency Index is the weighted mean of approved proficiency divided by five, multiplied by 100. Required levels and gaps are shown separately from the index.

Missing values do not become zeroes or incur a penalty. An incomplete index shows Insufficient Data; no supported values show Not Assessed. A not-applicable KPI requires a reason and is excluded from the calculation. No automatic penalty is applied for missing assignments or external delays.

## Assess and review

Choose an employee, period and matching role model. Record supported proficiency and KPI values, notes, and links to existing projects, tasks, documents or TechShare articles. Assessment-level links are reused by dimensions to reduce repeated entry. Choose an independent reviewer who is authorized for that employee. The employee and revision preparer cannot approve their own revision.

Save the draft, then submit it for review. The assigned reviewer records comments and either approves or requests changes. Every save creates an immutable revision with its role model, period, definitions and calculation snapshot. The dashboard uses only approved revisions. A later draft or configuration change does not replace earlier approved results. Audits record preparation, submission and decisions.

Employees can access their own confidential assessment. Admin, reporting managers and assigned team administrators can access their authorized people. Public project visibility does not grant access to confidential Praxis assessments. Native linked documents retain their own access controls.

## Explore and export

My Praxis shows separate indices, assessment coverage, goals and existing project/task experience. The matrix and charts open competency details with criteria, weights, source work, review comments, history and development recommendations. Team Insights supports department, team, role, employee, project and period filters. It displays coverage, advanced/expert proficiency and gaps without ranking employees.

Development goals may link to an existing project or task. Completing a goal updates development progress; approved proficiency changes only through assessment review. Existing work records and contribution review remain available through the experience profile. TechShare retains its existing library, contributor profiles and review workflows.

Reports offers Individual Performance & Capability, Competency Matrix, Development Progress, Team Capability and Department Skill Gap exports. PDF contains vector charts and summaries. Excel contains structured filterable sheets. Both use the dashboard calculations and include period, targets, methodology, references, reviewer details and generation date. Team reports respect manager scope and selected filters.
